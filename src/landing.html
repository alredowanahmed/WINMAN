Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Wingman Bro different from general chat AI? ▾
General AI writes formal, essay-like responses that feel unnatural in dating chats. Wingman Bro is calibrated strictly for real dating apps: short, witty, punchy messages that match her energy and make replying fun.
Are my screenshots and private messages saved? ▾
No. All conversation history is saved exclusively inside your browser's private local IndexedDB. We do not store your chat screenshots or conversation text on remote databases.
How do I navigate between the Landing page and the Generator Tool? ▾
You can click "Launch Tool" in the top menu or hero button anytime to navigate directly to /tool without any hash anchors or page reloads. In the tool, click "Home" to return to the landing page.
Can I use my own Google AI Studio API key? ▾
Yes! You can paste your own Gemini API key inside the app Settings. It will be stored locally in your browser for free, unlimited generations.
